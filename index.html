How the board is built

Confirmed — pulled by the scheduled agent

  • All YES bid / ask / last, 24h volume, open interest and close times from api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2 (series KXMLBGAME, KXBUNDESLIGAGAME, KXEFLCUPGAME, KXWNBAGAME), re-pulled every morning ~8:00a ET.
  • Win % = Kalshi mid normalized to 100% (2-way for MLB, 3-way incl. the tie for soccer). Entry = YES ask. Vig = win % minus entry.
  • Each day's five tickets are assembled by rule: highest-win-% liquid favorites for the chalk tickets, highest-volume favorites for Value 5, the MLB block plus a soccer leg or two for Longshot 6.
  • Ticket math is computed in-page from the legs: entry cost = product of asks, all-N-hit = product of no-vig win probs, EV = all-hit × payout − 1.

Assumptions & limits

  • “Fair” = de-vigged Kalshi mid, not an independent model. No injury / lineup / weather / pitching read is run.
  • Compounding basis. Kalshi does not auto-parlay these; separate YES contracts resolve independently and won't reproduce the quoted multiple.
  • Correlation not modeled. All-favorite tickets behave like one bet on a “favorite day”; EV assumes independence and is optimistic on variance.
  • The public share link does not auto-update. Each morning's republish is visible when you open the artifact; pushing it to everyone with the link still needs the share pin moved once per publish.
  • Lines move. Re-pull any leg at lineup lock / kickoff before you enter it.

Invalidation, global: skip or re-check a ticket if any leg’s YES ask is ≥3¢ worse than shown, a named MLB starter is scratched, a game is postponed, or a de-vigged price crosses 0.50 and flips the favorite.

Sizing, global: every ticket here is negative EV after spread. Treat as capped-risk entertainment. Suggested caps — 3-leg ≤1%, 4-leg ≤0.75%, 5-leg ≤0.4%, 6-leg ≤0.25% of prediction-market bankroll; ≤3–4% total across all open tickets.
